=== Standard CAFE Party Structure

Standard CAFE Forked Tower runs consist of:

* **4-6 Parties** (typically 6) - Party ABC, 123
* **Role Composition** - Standard is usually 2 tanks, 2 healers, 4 DPS per party
* **Phantom Job Preferences** - Have atleast 1 Phantom Jop at max-level
* **Side Assignments** - Left (A, B, C) vs Right (1, 2, 3) for positioning

TIP: Please check with your raid lead for any specific requirements or variations in structure for their run. Some may have different party compositions or special rules.
