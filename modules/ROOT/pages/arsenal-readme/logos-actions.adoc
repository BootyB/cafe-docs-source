You can see recommendations for which actions you should take depending on your class and how to use them in this image::tray-building.png[handy flowchart].

Also, in case you missed it during the Eureka story or tutorial, you can make two logos actions to bring with you into BA. It's highly recommended you bring a Spirit of The Remembered, a damage/healing/tank Wisdom, and a utility. Normally the Remembered is made by itself and popped first, before filling in the rest of your logos slots.

This is a tray. It's probably not the best tray for you, but it is a tray.
