== Unlocking Forked Tower Blood

To unlock The Forked Tower: Blood you will need to have completed the quest "Past and Crescent"

[link:https://www.reddit.com/r/ffxiv/comments/1kzdlp5/a_simple_guide_to_forked_tower_prep/]
image::ft-blood-prep.png[align center]

== Spawning Conditions


- 16 people of at least Knowledge Level 20

- 16 people have completed the story to unlock Forked Tower

- First spawn has a 5 minute internal timer.

- Subsequent spawns have a 60 minute internal timer

-- Timer starts when no Forked Tower is currently on going

-- Timer is reduced by 5 minutes when succesfully doing CEs

-- Timer is reduced by 1 minute when successfully doing FATEs (including pot fates)

When both conditions are met AND the internal timer reaches zero (either naturally or by being put below zero when completing FATEs/CEs) Forked Tower will spawn.

== Gearing Up

The relic gear you can make in The Occult Crescent does come with a boost in stats that is specific for this area. Upgrading your gear is recommended if you intend to clear or run the raid more than once.
