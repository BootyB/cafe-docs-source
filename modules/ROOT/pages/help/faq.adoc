== General

**Q: Can anyone join?**
Of course! CAFE is free and open for everyone!

**Q: Can I sign up for multiple runs at the same time?**

A: Yes! Use `/my-signups` to track all your commitments. Just make sure times don't overlap.

**Q: Can I change my role selection after signing up?**

A: Yes, just click the dropdown again in the thread or use `/my-signups` to update your roles. Be sure to reach out to your Raid Lead when you do as a courtesy.

**Q: How do I know if I got into a run?**

A: Check the signup status embed in the run thread, or use `/my-signups`. You'll see "Active" or "Reserve #X". In the case of BA, you'll know you got in when you join a party in Party Finder after the passcode drops. Lightning hands may be required!

**Q: What if I'm on reserve and the run fills up?**

A: Stay on reserve! People drop out frequently. Check the thread and the raid lead may open up more spots.

**Q: What if I sign up but can't make it?**

A: Withdraw ASAP using `/my-signups` or the thread's "Remove Signup" button.

**Q: Can I be blacklisted from signups?**

A: Yes, repeated no-shows or disruptive behavior can result in signup restrictions. Contact staff if you believe this was made in error.


== Roster Manager

**Q: How do Party Friends work?**

A: Set them with either `/forked-preferences` or `/drs-preferences`. Raid leads can see this info when using the Roster Manager, and the automatic distribution system takes it into account.

**Q: Can I request a specific party?**

A: Absolutely! Use either `/forked-preferences`  `/drs-preferences` to set your preferred party. Raid leads can see this info when using the Roster Manager, and the automatic distribution system takes it into account.

**Q: When are rosters distributed?**

A: Raid leads usually post rosters 1-6 hours before run time. The final roster is distributed with the passcode ping.

**Q: Can I get this on my Discord Server?**
Right now this is a CAFE Exclusive. You're always welcome to host runs on CAFE though!
